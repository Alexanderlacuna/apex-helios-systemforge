Architecture

Decisions made on paper, not discovered in production

Architecture is the set of decisions you cannot undo cheaply. We make them explicit, pressure-test them against your real constraints, and leave the reasoning behind — so your team can change the system later without guessing.

  1. 01

    Domain boundaries & decomposition

    Where services begin and end, which capability owns what, and what talks to what — mapped before code rather than reverse-engineered after it.

  2. 02

    Data modelling & ownership

    One owner per fact, explicit schemas and a migration path that survives growth — the difference between a system and a pile of tables.

  3. 03

    Integration contracts

    Published interfaces, versioning policy and event schemas, so changing one service cannot silently break three others.

  4. 04

    Scaling strategy

    Capacity plans and bottlenecks identified against real load assumptions, with the growth path stated up front instead of discovered at 3am.

  5. 05

    Failure modes & recovery

    What breaks first, what happens when it does, and what the recovery target is — idempotency and retries designed rather than hoped for.

  6. 06

    Documented decisions

    Architecture decision records and runbooks, so the reasoning stays with the system after the person who made the call has moved on.

AI infrastructure

Private AI, running on infrastructure you control

An API call is not a system. We build the layer underneath it — model serving, retrieval over your own data, orchestration, evaluation, and the pipelines that keep it fed — so AI features run on infrastructure you own and cost you can predict.

  1. 01

    Model serving & inference

    Open-weight models served on your own hardware or private VPC — quantised, batched, cached and load-tested, instead of a per-token meter running in the background.

  2. 02

    Retrieval over private data

    Ingestion, chunking, embeddings and hybrid search across your documents and databases, so answers are grounded in your records before they reach a model.

  3. 03

    Orchestration & automated systems

    Tool-calling pipelines and automated workflows with explicit state, retries and human checkpoints — orchestrators that carry work end to end instead of a black box.

  4. 04

    Evaluation & guardrails

    Labelled test sets, regression runs and trace review on every prompt or model change. You get numbers that justify trusting the system in production.

  5. 05

    Compute, deployment & cost control

    Containerised deployment, autoscaling and capacity planning sized against real traffic — with data-residency and privacy constraints designed in, not bolted on.

  6. 06

    Data pipelines underneath it

    Scheduled ingestion, transformation and sync jobs that keep retrieval fresh and traceable, with schema evolution handled deliberately.

About

Engineered for anywhere.

APEX HELIOS SYSTEMFORGE is a systems engineering studio working where architectural decisions meet artificial intelligence. A model is only as useful as the system that feeds it, and a system is only as sound as the decisions written down before it was built.

Most AI projects do not fail inside the model. They fail above it — data nobody owns, retrieval nobody trusts, and no way to tell whether an answer is right. Our work is the layer that makes the model accountable to your business.

We take a small number of engagements at a time. That is deliberate — architecture is not improved by being rushed.
