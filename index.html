AI infrastructure

Private AI, running on infrastructure you control

An API call is not a system. We build the layer underneath it — model serving, retrieval over your own data, orchestration, evaluation, and the pipelines that keep it fed — so AI features run on infrastructure you own and cost you can predict.

  1. 01

    Model serving & inference

    Open-weight models served on your own hardware or private VPC — quantised, batched, cached and load-tested, instead of a per-token meter running in the background.

  2. 02

    Retrieval over private data

    Ingestion, chunking, embeddings and hybrid search across your documents and databases, so answers are grounded in your records before they reach a model.

  3. 03

    Orchestration & agents

    Tool-calling pipelines and multi-step workflows with explicit state, retries and human checkpoints — bounded automation, not a black box.

  4. 04

    Evaluation & guardrails

    Labelled test sets, regression runs and trace review on every prompt or model change. You get numbers that justify trusting the system in production.

  5. 05

    Compute, deployment & cost control

    Containerised deployment, autoscaling and capacity planning sized against real traffic — with data-residency and privacy constraints designed in, not bolted on.

  6. 06

    Data pipelines underneath it

    Scheduled ingestion, transformation and sync jobs that keep retrieval fresh and traceable, with schema evolution handled deliberately.
